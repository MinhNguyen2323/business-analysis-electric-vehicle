# 🛵 Electric Vehicle Sales & Customer Care | Business Analysis

**Business Analysis | Process Optimization | Requirements Engineering | Digital Transformation**

## 📌 Overview

How can a traditional retailer improve operational efficiency without losing the personal touch that keeps customers coming back?

This project examines the sales and after-sales operations of **Xe Điện Lan Anh**, a Vietnamese electric vehicle retailer established in 2009.

Working with real business information, our team analyzed existing workflows, identified operational bottlenecks, and proposed a more integrated, customer-centric management system.

**Project objectives:**
- Understand and evaluate existing business processes (As-Is).
- Identify inefficiencies and their underlying causes.
- Redesign workflows and define system requirements (To-Be).
- Model a proposed solution and develop a website prototype.

## 🏢 Business Context

Xe Điện Lan Anh operates in electric vehicle retail, maintenance, and customer support, serving customers primarily through physical stores in Hanoi.

Its operations combine traditional procedures with basic digital tools, creating opportunities to improve information management, cross-department coordination, and customer experience.

The challenge was not simply to introduce new technology, but to determine **where digital transformation could create meaningful business value**.

## 🔍 Business Analysis Approach

### 1. Understanding the Current State (As-Is)

We examined the customer journey, from purchasing a vehicle to receiving warranty services and ongoing maintenance support.

**Key challenges identified:**
- Manual and fragmented customer information management.
- Limited automation of maintenance reminders and follow-ups.
- Inconsistent collection and tracking of customer feedback.
- Inefficiencies in inventory visibility and operational coordination.

**Analysis frameworks:** Rich Picture, BPMN, Fishbone Diagram, POPIT, and VMOST.

### 2. Stakeholder Analysis

Identified and evaluated **11 internal and external stakeholders**, including customers, sales teams, technicians, management, suppliers, and regulatory bodies.

Applied the **Power/Interest Grid** to understand stakeholder priorities and support requirements throughout the proposed transformation.

### 3. Designing the Future State (To-Be)

Proposed an integrated business management system connecting sales, customer service, inventory, and maintenance operations.

**Key improvements:**
- Centralized customer records and CRM capabilities.
- Automated maintenance reminders and after-sales communication.
- Improved sales and inventory information management.
- Structured customer feedback collection.
- Management dashboards for monitoring operational performance.

### 4. Requirements Engineering & Prototyping

Translated business needs into functional and non-functional requirements, supported by process and system models.

Developed a conceptual website prototype illustrating customer-facing and employee-facing workflows.

**Key takeaway:** Effective digital transformation begins with understanding business processes and stakeholder needs, not simply adding more technology.

## ⚖️ Strengths & Limitations

**Strengths**
- Grounded in an actual business context rather than a purely hypothetical case.
- Combines business strategy, process analysis, and system modeling.
- Connects operational problems to actionable system requirements.
- Considers both technical feasibility and customer experience.

**Limitations**
- The proposed system remains at the design and prototype stage.
- Operational improvements have not been quantitatively validated after implementation.
- Advanced features such as computer vision inspection, virtual showrooms, and automated danger alerts remain future concepts.
